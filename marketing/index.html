Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Do I need to buy special hardware?

No. Sillara runs in the browser on any phone, tablet, laptop or Android POS terminal you already own. If you want to print, any standard 80mm thermal printer works.

What happens when the internet goes down?

You keep selling. Sales are saved on your device and sync automatically when the connection returns — you'll see a small counter until everything is safely uploaded.

How do you charge? Can I cancel?

Monthly, cancel anytime, no contracts. Worldwide payments are handled by Paddle (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay); in Sri Lanka you can also pay with local cards via PayHere. The Free plan needs no card at all.

Is my data safe? Can I leave?

Your sales history belongs to you — export it anytime. Data is encrypted in transit, backed up daily, and never sold to anyone.

I run more than one shop.

The Pro plan handles multiple branches under one account with combined reporting. For chains with more than 10 tills, talk to us: hello@sillara.com.
